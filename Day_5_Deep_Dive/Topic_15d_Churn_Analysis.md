# Topic 15d: Churn Analysis Basics

## What is Churn Analysis?
Churn analysis is the process of evaluating your company's customer loss rate in order to reduce it. "Churn" refers to customers who stop using a product or service during a given time frame.

## Why is it Important?
Acquiring new customers is generally more expensive than retaining existing ones. By identifying the factors that lead to churn, businesses can take proactive steps to improve retention.

## Basic Steps in Churn Analysis
1. **Data Collection**: Gathering data on customer behavior, demographics, and transaction history.
2. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**: Looking for patterns. E.g., Do users on month-to-month contracts churn more often than those on yearly contracts?
3. **Defining the Churn Metric**: Calculating the basic churn rate (Customers Lost / Total Customers).
4. **Predictive Modeling** (Advanced): Using Machine Learning to predict *which* current customers are most likely to churn in the future.

## Analyzing Churn with Pandas
You can easily group customer data by their "Churn" status (Yes/No) and analyze differences in average spending, tenure, or other metrics.
